# External legal protection next steps

These actions cannot be completed or truthfully claimed through repository code alone. They require qualified professional or rights-holder action.

## Software copyright

Consider formal software copyright registration in relevant jurisdictions. Preserve repository history, release attestations, checksums, design records, contracts, and dated source archives as supporting evidence.

## Trademarks and brand assets

Ask qualified trademark counsel to assess and, where appropriate, register the OpenTrade word mark, official logo, and distinctive mascot or visual marks. The repository policy does not mean a registration already exists.

## Contributor and employment agreements

Confirm that employee, contractor, designer, and agency agreements assign or license the necessary software and design rights. DCO sign-off establishes submission authority but does not grant commercial relicensing rights; use a lawyer-reviewed contributor agreement before commercially relicensing external contributions.

## Visual provenance

Resolve the two JPEG illustration entries flagged in `docs/ownership-audit.md`. Preserve original Figma/design files, exports, timestamps, creator invoices, stock licences, generation records, and assignment agreements.

## Enforcement

The private monitoring repository contains a human-reviewed enforcement runbook. No automated finding is a legal conclusion. A qualified reviewer must verify ownership, licence scope, evidence integrity, jurisdiction, and proportionality before a formal notice, platform report, trademark complaint, or litigation step.
