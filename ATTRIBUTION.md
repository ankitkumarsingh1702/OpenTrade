# OpenTrade attribution

This document separates licence obligations from a voluntary acknowledgement request. It does not add restrictions to the GNU Affero General Public License.

## Legally required where applicable

When the applicable licence requires it, users and redistributors must:

- preserve the complete applicable licence and copyright notices;
- preserve relevant legal and third-party notices;
- provide Corresponding Source for qualifying distributions and modified network deployments;
- identify significant modifications and their dates;
- preserve third-party attribution and licence material; and
- avoid representing modified versions as the official OpenTrade product.

The exact obligations depend on the way the software is used, modified, hosted, or distributed. Consult [LICENSE](LICENSE) and qualified counsel for a specific use case.

## Requested but voluntary acknowledgement

Where an additional acknowledgement is appropriate, the project requests:

> This product uses software derived from OpenTrade, originally developed by Ankit Kumar Singh.
>
> Canonical source: <https://github.com/ankitkumarsingh1702/OpenTrade>

This acknowledgement is voluntary unless a separate written agreement makes it a requirement. The community licence does not require an OpenTrade credit on every application screen.
