OpenTrade brand indicia include, where legally protectable:

- the OpenTrade name and word mark;
- the OpenTrade logo and dog identity;
- mascots, product illustrations, and marketing artwork;
- distinctive product and game names;
- OpenTrade domain names and social identities; and
- the coordinated visual identity used to identify the official product.

Without separate permission, do not:

- present a modified project as the official OpenTrade product;
- use the OpenTrade logo as another product's primary logo;
- suggest sponsorship, endorsement, or affiliation;
- use confusingly similar branding; or
- reuse protected marketing artwork outside the permission that accompanied it.

Truthful descriptive references such as “Forked from OpenTrade,” “Built using OpenTrade,” and “Compatible with OpenTrade” are permitted when they are accurate, not misleading, and do not imply endorsement.

It does not claim that a trademark registration has been obtained, and it does not convert unverified third-party artwork into OpenTrade-owned material.
