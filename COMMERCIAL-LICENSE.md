# Alternative commercial licensing

Alternative commercial terms may be available for organisations that want rights beyond those already granted by `AGPL-3.0-or-later`, including:

- closed-source modifications or network deployments;
- white-label deployments;
- commercial redistribution under alternative terms;
- private customisation and dedicated support; or
- negotiated OpenTrade branding permission.

A commercial licence is not required for activity already permitted by the community licence. It does not automatically cover third-party packages, fonts, visual references, or other material that the copyright owner does not control.

At present, the repository history contains no merged external human contribution. Future external contributions are accepted under the community licence through the Developer Certificate of Origin. They are not automatically available for commercial relicensing unless a separate contributor agreement grants the necessary rights. A commercial offering must therefore be limited to owner-controlled material and contributions covered by appropriate written agreements.

For licensing enquiries, contact the repository owner through <https://github.com/ankitkumarsingh1702> or open an issue at <https://github.com/ankitkumarsingh1702/OpenTrade/issues/new>. Do not place confidential commercial information in a public issue.

No commercial licence or branding permission is granted until a separate written agreement is executed by the relevant rights holder.
